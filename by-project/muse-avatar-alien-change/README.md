# muse-avatar-alien-change

Avatar redesign project for the Muse assistant (October 2026). The assistant's
avatar was changed from "fluffy" — a cream-colored, yeti-like fluffy creature —
into "alien boy": a small green alien wearing a backwards baseball cap and a
white t-shirt with a red heart and white "linds" lettering.

## Characters

- **Fluffy** (before): a round, cream/beige fluffy creature, soft fur, simple
  smiling face. The original avatar.
- **Alien boy** (after): a small mint-green alien with large black oval eyes,
  wearing a dark backwards baseball cap (later iterations: sideways) and a white
  tee with a red heart. The active avatar.

## Process

The redesign iterated fast: 4 body-shape options, then 7 rounds of cap/tee
variations (cap backwards → cap + tee with outline heart → red heart → cap
sideways → cap sideways + tee → two more tee refinements), landing on the final
alien. A change-animation (fluffy holding a spinning glowing orb) marked the
transition.
